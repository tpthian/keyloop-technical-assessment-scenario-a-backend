# ADR-012: Scope discipline and demonstration surface

Status: Accepted, 2026-11-03 (D-005, D-007)

## Context
The brief is explicit about scope, and over-building reads as poor judgement.

## Decision
Cover the challenge exactly. Extra ideas go in design.md under future work, not in code. Tests prove the core business logic and acceptance criteria only. The demonstration surface is Swagger UI at `/docs`, `scripts/demo.sh` (including a race of 8 concurrent bookings for one slot, 2 winners on the seeded 2 bays x 2 technicians) and curl in the README. No custom frontend.

## Alternatives rejected
- Small custom frontend: breaks scope discipline, costs about half a day, and cannot show the concurrency guarantee.

## Consequences
Time goes to the design, the guarantee and the video. Out of scope: cancellation, rescheduling, admin APIs, notifications.
